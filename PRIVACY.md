# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-15_

Noter has no analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and no server of its own.

## What the app sends, and to whom

- **Notion only (api.notion.com).** Noter uses your Notion integration key to read the databases and
  pages you shared with that integration, and to create, edit and trash notes (pages) in them.
  Nothing is sent anywhere else.
- Links you open from the app (a note in Notion, a URL property) open in your browser or the Notion
  app, under their own privacy policies.

## What the app stores on your phone

- **Your Notion API key**, encrypted with the Android Keystore. If encryption isn't available, it is
  kept unencrypted in the app's private storage so the app keeps working. It is never backed up.
- **A copy of your notes** (properties and content of the databases you added) and changes not yet
  sent to Notion, so the app opens instantly and works offline. Kept in the app's private storage and
  never backed up. Removing the API key in Settings deletes this copy.
- **Your settings**: the databases you added (name, icon, chip property), pinned notes, note order
  and theme. These may be included in Android's own backup (to your Google account, if you turned that
  on) and in a transfer to a new phone.

Uninstalling the app deletes everything on the phone. Your notes in Notion are not affected.

## Permissions

- **Internet**: to talk to Notion.

If this changes, this file will be updated **before** the change is released.
